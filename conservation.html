Conservation Tips

Discover practical ways to conserve water, save energy, protect forests, and preserve biodiversity.

Protect What Matters

Natural resources support life on Earth. Small actions taken consistently can help protect them.

💧

Conserve Water

Freshwater is essential for people, animals, and plants. Use it wisely every day.

  • Turn off taps while brushing your teeth.
  • Repair leaking taps and pipes.
  • Use rainwater harvesting where practical.
  • Water plants when evaporation is lower.
⚡

Save Energy

Using electricity efficiently can reduce resource consumption and emissions.

  • Switch off unused lights and appliances.
  • Choose energy-efficient LED bulbs.
  • Use natural light whenever possible.
  • Choose public transport, cycling, or walking when suitable.
