Eco-Friendly Practices

Make sustainable choices in your daily life and help create a cleaner, healthier environment.

Simple Habits, Positive Impact

You don't need to change everything at once. Start with a few practical habits and build from there.

🛍️

Choose Reusable Products

Reduce single-use waste by choosing durable products.

  • Carry a reusable shopping bag.
  • Use a refillable water bottle.
  • Avoid unnecessary disposable items.
🚲

Travel Sustainably

Choose travel options that can reduce fuel use and pollution.

  • Walk or cycle for short trips.
  • Use public transport when practical.
  • Share rides to reduce separate journeys.
🥗

Reduce Food Waste

Plan meals and use food thoughtfully to avoid unnecessary waste.

  • Make a shopping list before buying food.
  • Store food properly.
  • Compost suitable food scraps where possible.
🪴

Grow Green Spaces

Support greenery and local biodiversity in your surroundings.

  • Grow suitable plants at home.
  • Choose native plants where appropriate.
  • Care for community gardens and green spaces.
💡

Save Energy at Home

Use electricity carefully and avoid unnecessary energy consumption.

  • Turn off unused lights and appliances.
  • Use daylight when available.
  • Choose energy-efficient equipment.
🤝

Inspire Your Community

Environmental protection becomes stronger when people work together.

  • Join local cleanliness drives.
  • Share reliable environmental information.
  • Encourage responsible habits at college and home.
