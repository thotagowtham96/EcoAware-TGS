Know your waste

Different Types of Waste

Correct sorting helps recover useful materials and reduces environmental pollution.

Organic waste

Wet Waste

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps and suitable food leftovers can be composted instead of being sent to landfill.

Reuse and recycle

Dry Waste

Separate accepted paper, cardboard, metal, glass and plastic for collection and recycling according to local rules.

Electronic waste

E-Waste

Old computers, mobile phones, chargers, cables and electronic devices need safe collection and authorized recycling.
